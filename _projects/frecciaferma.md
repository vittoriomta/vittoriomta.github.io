I don't have a car, so I take a lot of trains, and I kept wondering which one to catch to be less likely to arrive late. The sites I found, like [ViaggiaTreno](https://www.viaggiatreno.it/infomobilita/index.jsp) and Trenitalia's [Infomobilità](https://www.trenitalia.com/it/informazioni/Infomobilita.html), tell you how late a train is once you're already on it. None of them compare the trains on a route before you pick one. So I made one.

[Frecciaferma](https://frecciaferma.web.app) tells you which train to take. Type where you're going from and to, and it shows how often each train on that route arrives on time, and which one to avoid.

It's built on every train that has run in Italy since 2022, almost twelve million journeys. For the next few days it also weighs how the line is doing right now and whether heavy rain is forecast along the way.

The data comes from [TrainStats](https://github.com/emanu37429/trainstats), by [emanu37429](https://github.com/emanu37429). Thanks.

The site is in Italian. [Open it](https://frecciaferma.web.app).
